Privacy Policy - Mini Heist 3D

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Heist 3D collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection

Mini Heist 3D does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying personal information to play the game.

However, third-party advertising services integrated into the application may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information and operating system version.
  • Advertising identifiers, including Google Advertising ID.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Application usage and interaction data.
  • Advertisement impressions, clicks, and video views.
  • Crash logs, performance information, and diagnostic data.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining application functionality.
  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Delivering personalized or non-personalized advertising, subject to applicable privacy requirements.
  • Measuring advertising performance.
  • Improving application performance and stability.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Mini Heist 3D uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application.

The application includes the following advertising formats:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Interstitial advertisements.
  • Rewarded video advertisements.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and may provide in-game benefits, including character revival, additional virtual currency, and in-game equipment.

Google AdMob may collect, process, and share information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Apps That Use Its Services
  • Google Advertising Technologies

4. Consent and GDPR

Mini Heist 3D uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent requirements.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions may be presented with privacy choices in accordance with applicable laws.

Where required, users can review or modify their consent preferences through the application's privacy options.

Users may also manage advertising preferences through their device settings or Google's advertising settings:

Google My Ad Center

Where applicable, personal information is processed based on consent, legitimate interests, or other lawful grounds permitted by applicable legislation.

5. Local Game Data

The application stores certain gameplay information locally on the user's device, including:

  • Game progress.
  • Completed levels and difficulty settings.
  • Virtual currency balances.
  • Acquired weapons and equipment.
  • Character upgrades.
  • Game settings and preferences.

This information is used to preserve gameplay progress and provide application functionality.

Mini Heist 3D does not require an online player account or a cloud-based save service.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not directly sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or shared by third-party service providers, including Google AdMob, for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game information is retained to preserve the user's progress.

Users can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage through their device settings or uninstalling the application. Device backup settings may affect whether certain data is restored upon reinstallation.

Information collected by third-party services is retained in accordance with their own privacy policies, retention practices, and applicable legal requirements.

Users may contact the developer to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information under the developer's control.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Children's Privacy

Mini Heist 3D is not directed specifically toward children under the age of 13.

The developer does not knowingly request or collect directly identifying personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes that their child has provided personal information improperly, they may contact the developer to request appropriate action.

10. International Data Transfers

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located outside the user's country of residence.

Such processing is subject to applicable privacy laws and the relevant service provider's privacy practices.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Request correction or deletion.
  • Withdraw consent.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Submit a complaint to a relevant data protection authority.

Privacy-related requests can be submitted using the contact information below.
